Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. About the Application

PersonalFinance is a private, non-commercial application developed for personal financial management.

2. Data Collection

With the account holder's authorization, PersonalFinance retrieves bank account information, balances and transaction history through the Enable Banking API.

3. Data Usage

Banking information is used exclusively for personal expense tracking, financial analysis and budgeting.

4. Data Storage

Retrieved financial data is stored in a private database controlled by the application owner. Data is not sold or shared for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

PersonalFinance uses Enable Banking to access banking information with the account holder's consent.

6. Data Access and Deletion

The account holder may request access to or deletion of stored personal financial data by contacting the application owner. Banking authorization can be revoked through the relevant banking provider.
